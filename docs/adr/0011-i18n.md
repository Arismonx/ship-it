# ADR-0011: Separately configurable languages

**Status:** accepted (user choice: "everything configurable")

`lang.ui`, `lang.commits`, `lang.pr`, `lang.changelog` are independent. Defaults (revised on 2026-10-22, N15): **everything in English**, with a professional tone at the level of an open-source agency; French is available for every setting.
Embedded message catalogues (`embed`), fr and en in v1. The deterministic plan can generate its subjects in fr and en (per-language templates).
